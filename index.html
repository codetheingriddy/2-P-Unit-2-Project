The Tale of Squishy

Squishy was a young boy by the name of Squishy. He was just 7 years old when he grew a beard. This beard had a secret potion that turned his hair bright yellow. When he went to middle school, he was bullied for his mature look. He revolted, and became a superhero by the name of Bunny Foo Foo. DOE was so inspired that they put him on the background of every computer in the world.
